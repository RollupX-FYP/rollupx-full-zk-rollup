% While prior work provides insight into deployed rollups, this project focuses on controlled, configuration-level experimentation. Most benchmarking studies \cite{chaliasos2024analyzing} passively observe public production networks like Starknet or zkSync. Because these production systems prioritize reliability and full EVM compatibility, their core sequencing logic and DA selection cannot be easily toggled for controlled A/B testing. 

In contrast, our prototype prioritizes controlled experimentation, modularity, and observability. This approach provides an environment to conduct repeatable, isolated studies to observe exactly how individual design choices such as batch size limits, priority scheduling heuristics, or DA mode selection affect the throughput,latency,cost behavior without the noise of production mainnet traffic.
