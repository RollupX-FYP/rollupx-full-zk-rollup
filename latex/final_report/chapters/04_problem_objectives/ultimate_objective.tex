\section{Ultimate Objective}
\label{sec:ultimate_objective}

The ultimate objective of this research is to build a modular ZK-rollup benchmarking platform that enables researchers to experiment with new rollup design ideas at lower cost and with lower resource requirements than production rollup deployments. The framework should support reproducible validation of batching, sequencing, proving, and data availability strategies so that promising ideas can later inform production ZK-rollup systems and improve blockchain scalability and efficiency.
