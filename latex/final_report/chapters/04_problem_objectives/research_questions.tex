\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research_questions}
To guide this investigation, the project will answer the following research questions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{RQ1:} What is the quantitative impact of batch size on throughput, latency, proof-generation overhead, and per-transaction cost?
    \item \textbf{RQ2:} How do sequencer scheduling policies affect transaction ordering, fairness, latency, and execution correctness?
    \item \textbf{RQ3:} What are the cost implications of calldata, EIP-4844 blob DA, and offchain DA modes?
    \item \textbf{RQ4:} What practical bottlenecks emerge when implementing a modular research ZK-rollup prototype?
\end{enumerate}
These questions are designed to yield empirical, reproducible evidence to guide the design and optimization of ZK-Rollup systems.
