\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research_questions}
To guide this investigation, the project will answer the following research questions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{RQ1:} What is the quantitative impact of batch size on the throughput (TPS), proof generation latency, and per-transaction cost of a ZK-Rollup?
    \item \textbf{RQ2:} How do different sequencer scheduling policies (e.g., FIFO vs. priority fee) affect transaction confirmation times, particularly for high-value transactions under network congestion?
    \item \textbf{RQ3:} What are the cost and throughput implications of using different data availability mechanisms, specifically comparing on-chain calldata with EIP-4844 blobs?
\end{enumerate}
These questions are designed to yield empirical, reproducible evidence to guide the design and optimization of ZK-Rollup systems.
