\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research_questions}

To guide this investigation, the project will answer the following research questions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{RQ1:} How can a modular research-grade ZK-rollup benchmarking framework be designed to isolate key performance factors in the rollup pipeline?
    \item \textbf{RQ2:} How do batching, sequencing, proving, and data availability configurations affect ZK-rollup throughput, latency, finalization time, and cost?
    \item \textbf{RQ3:} What practical implementation bottlenecks and design limitations emerge when building and evaluating a reproducible ZK-rollup research prototype?
\end{enumerate}
