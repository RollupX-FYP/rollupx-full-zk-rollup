\section{The Throughput--Latency--Cost Trade-off}
\label{sec:throughput_latency_cost_tradeoff}
The empirical results observed in Stage 1 and Stage 5 clearly illustrate the fundamental throughput--latency--cost trade-off inherent in Layer-2 rollups. Optimizing for the absolute lowest Layer-1 cost per transaction dictates the use of exceptionally large batches to spread the fixed L1 submission and verification costs. However, this inherently penalizes early-arriving users with high queueing latency as they wait for the batch to fill. Conversely, providing fast soft finality requires smaller, rapidly sealed batches triggered by low timeouts, which drives up the amortized L1 cost per transaction.
