

\section{Prototype vs. Production Distinctions}
\label{sec:prototype_vs_production}

To correctly interpret the scope of the proposed framework, it is necessary to distinguish the research prototype from a production-grade mainnet ZK-rollup. The prototype is not intended to compete with deployed rollup systems or provide real-world security guarantees. Instead, it is designed as a controlled benchmarking environment for studying how selected rollup design choices affect throughput, latency, finalization time, proving overhead, and data availability cost. Table~\ref{tab:prototype_vs_production} summarizes the main distinctions.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering
\caption{Prototype vs. Production Rollup Architecture}
\label{tab:prototype_vs_production}
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.18}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.18\textwidth}Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Aspect} & \textbf{Production rollup} & \textbf{This research prototype} \\
\midrule

Execution 
& Supports full or near-full EVM compatibility for general smart contracts. 
& Uses a simplified transfer-centric state-transition engine for controlled workloads. \\

\addlinespace

Proving 
& Uses optimized production proving infrastructure, often with recursion and parallelization. 
& Uses mock proving delays and selected RISC0 experiments to isolate prover bottlenecks. \\

\addlinespace

Sequencing 
& Designed for high availability, fault tolerance, and real user demand. 
& Uses a configurable local sequencer to test batching and ordering policies. \\

\addlinespace

Layer 1 
& Runs against public networks where gas prices and congestion vary. 
& Runs against a local Hardhat Layer-1 node to improve repeatability. \\

\addlinespace

Data availability 
& Uses mainnet-compatible DA mechanisms such as calldata, blobs, or external DA layers. 
& Evaluates calldata, EIP-4844 blob-style submission, and a local off-chain DA stub. \\

\addlinespace

Security 
& Must provide safety, liveness, and data availability guarantees in adversarial settings. 
& Runs in a controlled, non-Byzantine environment and does not claim production security. \\

\addlinespace

Objective 
& Provides secure scaling infrastructure for real users and applications. 
& Provides a reproducible benchmarking framework for ZK-rollup trade-off analysis. \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\end{table}

These distinctions define the intended contribution of the project. The value of the prototype lies in its ability to isolate and measure specific design variables rather than in providing a complete production rollup implementation. By simplifying execution, controlling the Layer-1 environment, and exposing configurable components, the framework allows systematic experiments on batching, sequencing, proving, and data availability choices.
