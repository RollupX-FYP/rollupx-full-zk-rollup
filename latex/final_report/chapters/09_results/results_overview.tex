This chapter presents the findings from the automated benchmark runs (Stage 1 through Stage 5). It is important to emphasize that these results are derived from a \textit{controlled benchmarking prototype} using synthetic workloads and parameterized delays. Therefore, the absolute numbers represent a configuration-level, relative comparison of trade-offs rather than a claim of production mainnet performance.
\subsection{Overview of Evaluation Stages}
The empirical results are structured across five distinct stages:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1: Batch Size, Cost, and Latency.} Evaluates the fundamental latency-cost trade-off using fixed batch sizes and timeouts.
    \item \textbf{Stage 2: Adaptive Batching.} Evaluates dynamic batch scaling based on mempool load and threshold settings, validating the latency-reduction capabilities of adaptive batching.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3: Sequencer Scheduling Policies.} Evaluates the effect of transaction ordering policies (such as FCFS, FairBFT, FeePriority, and TimeBoost) on fairness and throughput under a nonce-aware scheduler design.
    \item \textbf{Stage 4: Data Availability (DA) Modes.} Analyzes the cost savings of Calldata, Blob (EIP-4844), and Off-chain DA modes.
    \item \textbf{Stage 5: RISC0 Proving.} Profiles the cycle behavior, segment boundaries, and fixed overheads of cryptographic proof generation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Validation of Scheduling and Batching Designs}
The empirical results successfully validated the key components of the modular ZK-Rollup prototype:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nonce-Aware Scheduler Validation:} Under the benchmark runs, the priority scheduling policies (\texttt{FeePriority} and \texttt{TimeBoost})—which group transactions by account and sort them strictly by nonce—executed all transactions successfully with 100\% transaction execution success (0 empty batches), yielding FCFS-equivalent regular gas costs (~20.2k gas/tx).
    \item \textbf{Adaptive Batching Performance:} Under low offered load (5 TPS), the adaptive batching policy successfully reduced mempool queue latency to 252 ms while maintaining gas amortization efficiency.
    \item \textbf{Production zkVM Prover Profiling:} Real RISC0 prover sweeps validated the computational proving bottlenecks, demonstrating linear cycle scaling with segment-based step-function proving delays and a constant 256-byte proof footprint.
\end{itemize}
