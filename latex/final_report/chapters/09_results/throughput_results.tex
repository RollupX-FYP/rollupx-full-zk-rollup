\section{Throughput Results}
\label{sec:throughput_results}
Initial measurements (Stage 1) captured the baseline throughput of the system. The synthetic Poisson generator successfully offered load to the sequencer up to 100 TPS. However, the committed throughput (measured at the L1 Contract level) was ultimately bounded by the L1 block confirmation time of the local Hardhat node (simulating a 12-second block time). Under heavy load, the sequencer correctly applied backpressure and batched transactions up to the defined \texttt{MAX\_BATCH\_SIZE} limits (e.g., 100).
