\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:intro_contributions}

The primary contributions of this project include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{C1:} A modular ZK-rollup benchmarking framework with separable sequencer, executor, prover, submitter, smart contract, and DA components.
    \item \textbf{C2:} A reproducible experimental pipeline with synthetic workload generation, Docker-based orchestration, configuration sweeps, and JSON/CSV telemetry collection.
    \item \textbf{C3:} Empirical findings on throughput-latency-cost trade-offs across batching, sequencing, DA modes, and prover configurations, including observed anomalies such as adaptive batching limitations, nonce ordering issues, blob cost reduction, and RISC0 proving step-function behavior.
\end{enumerate}
