\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:intro_contributions}

The primary contributions of this project include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The design and implementation of a modular ZK-rollup benchmarking prototype that strictly separates sequencing, execution, proving, and submission stages into distinct Rust microservices.
    \item A controlled experimental framework, including a Python-based Poisson workload generator, capable of executing reproducible configuration sweeps to measure throughput, latency, finalization time, and cost metrics.
    \item Empirical analysis identifying the critical throughput--latency--cost trade-offs across different batching parameters and Data Availability (DA) modes based on extensive local testnet benchmarking.
    \item The demonstration of both a benchmark-mode parameterized proof delay and a real \texttt{risc0} proof integration as a valid methodology for analyzing system-level queueing dynamics and fixed setup costs independently of full production zkEVM complexity.
\end{enumerate}
