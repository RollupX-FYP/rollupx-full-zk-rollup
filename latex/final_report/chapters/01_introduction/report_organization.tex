\section{Document Structure}
\label{sec:document_structure}

The remainder of this report is organized as follows. Chapter~2 reviews the related literature on blockchain scalability, rollup architectures, data availability, and ZK-Rollup benchmarking. Chapter~3 presents the research methodology, system design, configuration space, cost model, and evaluation metrics. Chapter~4 describes the implementation of the modular prototype, including the sequencer, executor, prover, submitter, smart contracts, and benchmarking tools. Chapter~5 presents the evaluation setup, experimental results, validated findings, and discussion of the observed throughput--latency--cost trade-offs. Finally, Chapter~6 concludes the report and outlines future work.
