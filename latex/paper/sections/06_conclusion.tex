\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents the design and evaluation of a modular benchmarking prototype for L2 ZK-rollups. Our results show that both EIP-4844 blobs and off-chain storage can significantly reduce the cost of L1 transactions relative to calldata transactions, again for sufficiently large transaction batch sizes. Furthermore, our implementation of an adaptive batching policy eliminates the high-queue-size latency of a fixed batching policy, while maintaining the low gas costs of an optimal batching policy at high throughput. Finally, profiling the off-chain proving step reveals that the cost of executing zkVM programs is a step function in the number of segments of a batch, indicating that the validity proving pipeline is the main constraint on the throughput of L2-to-L1 hard finality proofs.
